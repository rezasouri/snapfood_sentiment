# NLP and ABSA feasibility

20,000 deterministic review samples were searched with transparent Persian keyword patterns. Counts are **candidate mentions**, not labels: taste/quality 7,808; temperature 2,285; portion 1,945; packaging 1,124; price/value 1,103; delivery delay 1,121; courier 950; service 515; missing/wrong item 279. The patterns can miss slang, negation and implicit complaints, and can produce false positives. Source and IDs for local inspection are in [`reports/text_analysis.json`](../reports/text_analysis.json); no raw text is published.

Manual inspection of 25 deterministic review records found short generic praise, multi-aspect comments, mixed positive/negative comments, colloquial spelling and negation. Examples included a high overall HAPPY score alongside a complaint about warm drinks and another HAPPY review with criticism of one dessert but praise for another. This confirms that overall `feeling`/rating cannot substitute for aspect sentiment. Sarcasm and implicit complaint prevalence remain **unknown**; 25 records cannot quantify them.

Proposed extraction unit: `(review_id, aspect, sentiment, issue_category, evidence_span, confidence_or_uncertainty, label_source)`. Allow multiple rows per review, `mixed`/`uncertain` sentiment, and `no_issue`. Preserve offsets to the original text. Existing SnappFood labels are only overall `feeling` and rating; every aspect, issue and evidence span must be generated or imported. Under the current **no manual labeling** constraint, call generated labels *silver labels*, retain model/prompt provenance, and never use them as ground truth.

ABSA is **conditionally feasible** as an exploratory feature. For independent evaluation, use existing manually annotated Persian benchmarks: [Pars-ABSA](https://github.com/Titowak/Pars-ABSA) contains aspect terms, offsets and polarity for product reviews; [ParsiNLU sentiment](https://huggingface.co/datasets/persiannlp/parsinlu_sentiment) includes food-and-beverage aspects and sentiment. Neither is a gold SnappFood test set. Report benchmark scores as *out-of-domain* and report SnappFood issue counts as model estimates. Check coverage of the chosen food-delivery taxonomy; delivery and courier behavior may not be represented. Without a human-verified in-domain set, SnappFood ABSA precision/recall and issue-rate accuracy remain unknown. Rare categories and sarcasm may need to be collapsed or deferred.
